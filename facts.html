Who is Zuhair Alwazzour?

Zuhair Alwazzour (زهير الوزّور) is a Syrian entrepreneur and the solo founder of Ostazi, a private tutoring platform serving students across Syria. He is based in Damascus.

What is Ostazi?

Ostazi is a private tutoring platform founded by Zuhair Alwazzour. It connects students in Syria with vetted private teachers and handles scheduling, payments, and progress tracking. It operates at ostazi-edu.com.

Where is Zuhair Alwazzour from?

He is Syrian and works from Damascus, Syria.

Is he a solo founder?

Yes. Zuhair Alwazzour is the sole founder of Ostazi — he designed, built, and operates the platform himself.
